## Project Showcase
**Project Title**: Contrasting Seismicity and Population Exposure Between the Asia-Pacific and the US West Coast, 1990–2023
**Research Question**: How do seismic activity patterns and population earthquake exposure differ between the Asia-Pacific subduction zone and the US West Coast from 1990 to 2023?

![Project Visualisation](figures/seismic_map.png)
> *Brief explanation of findings*: The Asia-Pacific region hosts more frequent large earthquakes, while the US West Coast presents concentrated seismicity along transform faults. Population exposure varies significantly due to differences in urban distribution and settlement density across the two regions.
